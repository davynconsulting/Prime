Prime Insurance AI claims fraud, claims intake and bank reconciliation

Synthetic data, not real data

Three working desks built from the PRD and SRD. Every claim, email and bank line here is invented, so the numbers show how each engine behaves, not how it will perform on Prime's own data.

Phase 1

Fraud desk

Scores every claim with rules, peer-group anomalies and network links, sends the riskiest to investigators, and saves their outcomes as training labels.

On the synthetic back-test it caught 57 of 100 planted fraud cases at 5% referral capacity, against 20 for a red-flag checklist.

Phase 2

Claims intake

Reads first-notice-of-loss emails, checks cover, lists missing documents, routes each claim, and settles simple ones only when the phase 1 fraud score allows.

Reading with Claude needs an Anthropic API key on the server. Without one, the rules reading is used.

Phase 3

Reconciliation

Matches bank lines to premiums and claim payments, clears confident matches, queues the rest with suggestions, and sends payment problems to the fraud team.

On a synthetic month it cleared 87% of 1,574 bank lines automatically, with 0 wrong automatic matches.
